Frequently asked questions

Is QuickInvoice really free?

Yes. Creating and downloading invoices is completely free, with no limit on how many you make and no watermark on the PDF. There's no paid tier you're nudged toward.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No signup, no account, no email required. Open the page and start typing. That's the whole point — most invoice tools make you register before you can do anything.

Is my invoice data private?

Completely. QuickInvoice runs entirely in your browser. Your details are saved only to your own device's local storage and are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Clear your browser data and they're gone.

How do I download my invoice as a PDF?

Click Download PDF. Your browser's print dialog opens — choose “Save as PDF” as the destination and save. The invoice prints clean, without the editor around it.

Can I add my own logo and tax rate?

Yes. Upload a logo (it stays on your device) and set any tax rate or discount percentage. Totals recalculate instantly in the live preview.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The editor and preview adapt to small screens, and you can download the PDF from mobile browsers that support “Save as PDF” or printing.
