How to use this template

  1. Open the editor. The QuickInvoice generator loads with the same layout you see above and saves to your browser as you type.
  2. Replace the parties. Put your name and contact under From, and the client plus the person who approves payment under Bill to.
  3. Set the number and dates. Use a sequential number like 2026-015 and add a real due date — not just "due on receipt".
  4. List the work plainly. One line per item, with quantity and rate. Totals, tax, and discount calculate automatically.
  5. Add how to pay you and download the PDF. Attach it to a short, polite email.

Why this template gets you paid

  • The word "Invoice" up top, so it isn't mistaken for a quote.
  • A unique, sequential number that keeps both sides' books clean.
  • An explicit due date, so it doesn't drift to the bottom of the pile.
  • Itemised work the client can match to what they approved.
  • Clear payment instructions, so there's no back-and-forth before they can pay.
